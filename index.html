About this setup

This workspace is currently a front-end only app. Sign-in, server, database, payment processing, and owner/admin controls are not connected. No real payments, balances, tasks, or earnings are available.

Before launch, configure Supabase authentication and server-side payment verification, complete Stripe and M-Pesa merchant onboarding, designate the owner securely, and fund task rewards independently of user top-ups.
